THE PROBLEM TODAY

Liquidity needs lead to redemptions

When your investors need liquidity, redemption is often the only option. This risks losing capital from your fund.

What changes for your fund
Fewer redemptions.

Enable investors to borrow against their units rather than redeem. They remain invested, and your AUM is retained.

A stronger case for allocation.

Units that can serve as collateral are more valuable to investors, which strengthens the case for allocating to your fund.

A competitive differentiator.

Few private funds allow investors to borrow against their units without redeeming. Offering it differentiates your fund when investors compare options.

HOW IT WORKS

Pledged in minutes,
released on repayment

You approve the lenders and set the terms. Investors pledge units as security, and the units remain locked until the loan is repaid.

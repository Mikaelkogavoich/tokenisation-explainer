SECONDARY TRANSFER

Offer investors an exit, without a redemption.

Enable investors to exit between redemption windows by transferring their position to another eligible investor. Your fund's capital is unaffected.

THE PROBLEM TODAY

Lock-up periods that deter investors

In most private funds, investors can exit only at scheduled redemption windows, and meeting redemptions may require the sale of assets. For many investors, this lock-up is a key reason not to invest.

What changes for your fund
Capital stays in the fund.

Unlike a redemption, a secondary transfer does not draw on fund capital. The position moves to a new holder, and your investment strategy is preserved.

Fewer barriers to investment.

Investors commit more readily when an exit path is available. This addresses a common objection to private funds and supports AUM growth.

A structural advantage.

Liquidity between redemption windows is uncommon in private markets. Offering secondary transfers differentiates your fund from traditional operators.

HOW IT WORKS

Your rules, on every transfer

When two eligible investors agree to a transfer, ownership and payment are exchanged in a single step. Your eligibility rules and limits apply automatically, and every transfer is recorded on your register in real time.

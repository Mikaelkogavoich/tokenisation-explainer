How it works

From building to token in four steps

The asset is a commercial property fund: a unit trust that owns an office building. Tokenisation records ownership as digital tokens, and lets you program the rules of the asset. The fund stays a unit trust.

  1. 1

    Set up

    The fund manager verifies the entity, adds the fund details, and sets the rules on who can hold. Those rules are encoded into the token's smart contract.

  2. 2

    Investors

    Each investor completes identity checks and investor classification. No verified wallet, no tokens.

  3. 3

    Issue

    Tokens are minted on Redbelly Network and sent to verified wallets. Each is a fractional ownership interest, and the register updates immediately.

  4. 4

    Transfer

    Every transfer is checked against the rules first. A transfer to an ineligible investor is rejected. A compliant one settles in seconds.
